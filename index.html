Traditional Automation vs. Enterprise Agentic AI

Scripted RPA breaks the moment reality deviates from the flowchart. Agentic systems reason, retrieve context, and recover — with a human checkpoint wherever it matters.

Rigid & Fragile

Traditional Automation

  • Linear, rule‑based execution only
  • Fails silently on edge cases
  • No contextual reasoning or memory
  • Expensive to re‑script for every operational change

Agentic AI (Veridion)

  • Reasons over enterprise tools, databases, and retrieved context
  • Escalates to a human checkpoint on ambiguity
  • Adapts to new inputs without re‑engineering code
  • Self‑documents decisions for compliance audit trails
